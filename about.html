Our Genesis

Bridging the Trust Gap in Everyday Doorstep Care

In every Indian neighborhood, finding a dependable electrician on short notice, a trustworthy plumber during a pipe emergency, or a certified cleaner often turns into a frustrating ordeal filled with erratic rates, no-shows, and safety concerns.

At the same time, thousands of hardworking, skilled artisans—painters, carpenters, AC mechanics, and domestic helpers—struggle with sporadic income, predatory middlemen, and lack of professional dignity.

SANGI was born to solve both sides of this equation. By combining deep technology, rigorous police-verified KYC, transparent standard rate cards, and an accessible ₹30 base booking fee, SANGI turns irregular service hunts into a seamless 3-tap doorstep reality.
