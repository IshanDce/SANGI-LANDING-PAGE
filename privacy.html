Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026 • Effective Date: October 12, 2026

Summary: SANGI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. We collect only the data necessary to provide doorstep home services, verify service partners, and enable real-time booking fulfillment. We never sell your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use the SANGI mobile application or website, we may collect:

  • Account Details: Full name, phone number, email address, and profile photo.
  • Service Location & Address: Doorstep service address, landmark, and GPS coordinates provided for home service visits.
  • Service Partner / Technician Verification (KYC): Government identity proofs (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, profile photo) submitted exclusively for background verification and safety.
  • Payment & Transaction Information: Booking payment status, transaction reference IDs, and payout bank/UPI details. We do not store raw credit/debit card numbers or CVV; all digital payments are securely processed by RBI-licensed payment gateways (e.g. Razorpay).

2. Location Information & Foreground Services

SANGI provides on-demand doorstep services where accurate location is essential:

  • Customer Location: Used to display nearby verified service partners, calculate accurate travel distance, and navigate the service technician to your home address.
  • Service Partner (Technician) Live Tracking: For service technicians on duty, the app utilizes foreground location service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION) to broadcast real-time GPS coordinates while traveling to the booked job. This allows customers to track technician arrival in real-time. A persistent notification is displayed on the technician's device while tracking is running.
  • Location data is only collected when necessary for service discovery and active job fulfillment.

3. Camera and Storage Permissions

The app requests access to your device camera and storage solely for:

  • Uploading profile pictures.
  • Uploading Aadhaar and PAN documents for service partner KYC verification.
  • Uploading job completion or service requirement photos.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

  • Facilitate on-demand home service bookings and dispatch verified technicians.
  • Provide real-time booking status notifications via SMS and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Process service fee payments and partner payout withdrawals.
  • Ensure platform safety, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable laws.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share information with:

  • Assigned Service Partners / Customers: Customer name and address are shared with the assigned technician only after booking confirmation to enable service execution.
  • Authorized Service Providers: Cloud infrastructure (AWS), SMS gateways (Linerpay), and payment gateways (Razorpay) under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by applicable law, court order, or government regulation.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS / SSL / TLS), secure database access controls, and hashed passwords (bcrypt) to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

SANGI is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
