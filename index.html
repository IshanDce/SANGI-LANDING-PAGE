3. Platform Booking Fee & Pricing Policy

To maintain rigorous quality standards, ongoing background verification, emergency response operations, and insurance coverage, SANGI charges a standard nominal base booking fee of ₹30 (Thirty Rupees) for every confirmed service booking.

  • The ₹30 booking fee is payable upon scheduling the service to secure technician allocation.
  • Estimated labor charges for services (such as plumbing, electrical work, or AC maintenance) are provided upfront as transparent standard estimates.
  • Final service costs may adjust if the scope of work changes upon in-person diagnostic inspection with customer consent.
  • All spare parts or replacement hardware are billed separately at MRP with authentic retailer invoices.

4. Verification, Safety & OTP Protocol

SANGI subjects all service personnel to multi-tier identity checks, including government Aadhaar verification and criminal record background checks. For security, every service appointment generates a unique 4-digit One-Time Password (OTP) in the customer app. The technician cannot begin the service until the customer physically reveals the OTP at the doorstep.

5. Cancellation and Refund Policy

  • Free Cancellation: Customers may cancel or reschedule a scheduled booking free of charge up to 60 minutes before the scheduled time slot.
  • Booking Fee Refund: In the event of a timely cancellation, the ₹30 booking fee is credited back instantly to your SANGI Wallet or original payment method within 3 to 5 business days.
  • Late Cancellation: Cancellations made when the technician is already en route (within 30 minutes of arrival) may forfeit the ₹30 base fee to compensate the technician for transportation expenses.

6. 30-Day Quality Guarantee

We stand firmly behind our artisans' workmanship. All physical repairs and installations carry a 30-day SANGI Guarantee. If a diagnosed issue recurs under normal usage within 30 days of service completion, SANGI will arrange a free inspection and rectification.
